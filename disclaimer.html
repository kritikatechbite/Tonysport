Informational Purpose Only

Tony Sport Hub is an informational and editorial website. It publishes guides, explainers and event information about sports followed in Canada. The content is provided for general interest and education. It is not professional advice of any kind, whether legal, financial, medical, travel or otherwise, and it should not be used as a substitute for advice from a qualified person.

Nothing on the website is an offer, an invitation or a recommendation to take part in any activity, to buy any product or to make any decision that carries financial, legal or personal risk.

Sports News and Content Accuracy

We try to be accurate, and our Editorial Policy sets out the standards we follow. Even so, we cannot promise that every statement on the website is complete, current or free of mistakes. Rules change from season to season, league structures are reorganized, competitions are renamed, and clubs join or leave. A page that was correct on the date it was written may no longer be correct today.

If you rely on something you read here, such as a rule, a competition format or a league's membership, you do so at your own risk. We recommend that you confirm important details with the official source before acting on them.

Sample and Demonstration Content

A number of items on this website are samples. The fixtures on the matches page and in the matching panel on the home page use fictional teams, fictional competitions and example venues and dates. They are included to show how event information is laid out. They are not real events, and no real person, team or organization has played, or will play, in them.

Articles on the insights page are original explainers and sample editorial pieces. They describe how sports work in general, and they are not breaking news. Where an article or a listing is a sample, we say so on the page. Please do not treat any demonstration content as a report of something that has really happened.

Match Schedules and Event Changes

Sports schedules are never fixed in stone. Games can be postponed because of weather, safety concerns, venue problems, public health decisions, scheduling conflicts or decisions by leagues and organizers. Start times can move to suit broadcasters, and venues can change at short notice. Competitions can be cancelled.

If this website presents schedule or event information that is based on verified sources, it will be accurate only as of the time it was last updated. You should always check with the official organizer before you travel, make bookings, take time off work or make any other plans that depend on an event taking place at a certain time and place.

Time zones

Canada has six time zones. We try to label the time zone alongside every start time, but it is your responsibility to convert times to your own location. If a time zone is missing or unclear, please check with the organizer.

No Guarantee of Real-Time Updates

This website is not a live scoreboard and is not a news ticker. We do not provide real-time scores, play-by-play, live commentary, odds or push notifications. Pages are updated by the site owner from time to time, and a page may remain unchanged for days or weeks. The words "latest" and "upcoming" on this website describe the most recent content we have published, and they do not mean that the information is current to the minute.

For live results, use the official website, app or broadcast of the league or event you follow.

Statistics, Records and Historical Information

Statistics, records, dates and historical references on this website come from published rulebooks, official resources and other sources we consider reliable. Records are broken, rules are reinterpreted and official bodies sometimes revise historical figures. A fact that was correct when we wrote it may later be updated or disputed, and some historical details are described differently by different sources.

When a number matters to you, for example for a school project, a publication or a discussion where precision counts, please check it against the original official source. If we have made an error, we will correct it as described in our Editorial Policy.

For example, the rules of Canadian football differ from those of American football, and some competitions run under different names in different regions. If you are reading from outside Canada, please check local sources before relying on our content.
