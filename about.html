About Maple Sports Hub

Sports information, made clear

Maple Sports Hub is an independent website that publishes guides, explainers and event information about sports followed in Canada. It is an information resource only.

We do not offer betting, gambling, fantasy contests or ticket sales, and we are not affiliated with any professional league, team or governing body.

Our Purpose

Helping fans understand the games they love

Our purpose is to make sports easier to understand. We write about rules, formats, competitions and terminology so newcomers can follow along and long-time fans can find a useful reference.

What We Cover

Six sports with a Canadian focus

We cover ice hockey, basketball, soccer, Canadian football, baseball and tennis. Our sports guides explain how each sport works, and our insights section publishes explainers and analysis.

How We Prepare Information

Research, writing and review

Articles are written from published rulebooks, official league resources and general sports knowledge. Where details change often, such as schedules or league membership, we encourage readers to confirm with the official organization. Demonstration content is always labelled as such.

Editorial Standards

Independence and care

Our editorial choices are not influenced by teams, leagues or advertisers. Read the full Editorial Policy for details on sourcing, attribution and conflicts of interest.

Corrections and Updates

Getting it right

If you find an error, let us know using the contact information below. We correct factual errors promptly and note significant changes to an article.
