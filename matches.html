FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are these real fixtures?

No. Every event on this page is a clearly marked demonstration. We use fictional teams, venues and dates to show how event information is presented.

Why are there no scores or live updates?

This is an information site. It does not provide live scores, odds, wagering or streaming. For results, use the official league website.

How do I check a start time?

Look at the time zone label next to each time. Canada has six zones, and organizers can change start times, so confirm with the league or venue.

Can I buy tickets here?

No. Tony Sport Hub does not sell or book tickets. Tickets are handled by the event organizer or venue.

What does the event status mean?

'Example only' means the entry is a demonstration. When verified information is published, status labels such as 'Scheduled', 'Postponed' or 'Completed' will show the state of the event.
