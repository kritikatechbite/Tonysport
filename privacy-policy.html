Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tony Sport Hub handles information when you visit this website. Tony Sport Hub is an independent sports information site. It is built as a static website, which means it has no user accounts, no comment sections, no contact forms, no newsletters and no online payments. That design keeps the amount of information we handle small, and this policy describes what that looks like in practice.

Who This Policy Applies To

This policy applies to everyone who visits any page on Tony Sport Hub, including the home page, the sports, matches, insights and about pages, the information notice page and the policy pages. It does not apply to external websites that we link to. Those websites have their own privacy practices, and you should read them before sharing information with them.

Tony Sport Hub provides general sports information for readers in Canada. It is not directed at children, and it is not designed to collect personal information from anyone, whatever their age.

Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account, sign in, fill in a form, subscribe to a mailing list or provide any personal details to read the website. Because of this, we do not knowingly collect names, email addresses, telephone numbers, mailing addresses, payment information or similar details through the pages of this website.

If you choose to contact the site owner by email once contact details are published, the message and your email address will be received through your own email provider. We would use that information only to read and respond to your message, and to keep a record of corrections if your message concerns an error.

Information we do not collect

  • Account credentials, because there are no accounts
  • Payment card or banking information, because we do not sell anything
  • Precise location data, because the site does not request your location
  • Information from social media accounts, because the site does not use social sign-in
  • Sensitive personal information of any kind

Technical Data and Server Logs

Whenever you open a web page, your browser sends a request to the server that hosts the site. That request normally contains your internet protocol (IP) address, the date and time, the page requested, the type of browser and device you are using, and the page that referred you. Web hosting providers commonly keep this information in server logs for security, troubleshooting and basic operational statistics.

The hosting provider selected by the site owner is responsible for those logs. We do not use them to build profiles of individual visitors, and we do not combine them with other personal data. The retention period depends on the hosting provider's own settings, which the site owner should review and, if needed, describe in an update to this policy.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website, as delivered, does not set cookies of its own, and it does not use browser storage to keep personal data. Our Cookie Policy explains this in more detail, including what could change if we introduce new features in the future.

Your browser or your hosting provider may use technical mechanisms that are outside our control. For example, a content delivery network may set a security cookie to protect against abuse. If that happens, it is a function of the service provider rather than a feature we have added to the pages.

Analytics

The website does not include an analytics service in its current form. We do not run third-party scripts that track visitors across pages or across other websites, and we do not use advertising pixels or social media tracking tools.

If analytics are introduced later, for example to learn which pages are most useful, we will update this policy before or at the same time as the change. The update would name the service, describe the data it collects, explain whether it uses cookies and describe how you can opt out. Privacy-respecting, aggregated measurement would be our preference.

Third-Party Fonts and Resources

To display text in the intended typefaces, pages load fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those font files, it connects to servers operated by Google, and Google receives technical information such as your IP address and browser details. That exchange is governed by Google's own privacy documentation. If you prefer to avoid it, you can configure your browser to block third-party fonts, in which case the site will display in a default font and remain fully usable.

The site owner may choose to host the fonts directly on the same server in the future, which would remove this third-party request. If that change is made, this section will be updated.

How We Use Information

The limited information described above is used for a small set of purposes:

  • To deliver the pages you request and keep the website running
  • To protect the website and its hosting from abuse, errors and security threats
  • To understand in general terms whether the website works properly across devices and browsers
  • To respond to messages or corrections if you contact the site owner
  • To meet legal obligations if they arise

We do not sell personal information. We do not rent it, trade it, or use it to make automated decisions about you. We do not use it for targeted advertising.

Legal Basis and Consent

In Canada, privacy expectations rest on the idea that organizations should be open about what they collect and why, and should obtain meaningful consent where personal information is collected. Because this website collects very little, consent is generally implied by your choice to visit and use it. If a feature is added that collects more information, we will explain it clearly at the point of collection and ask for your agreement where required.

You can withdraw from this arrangement at any time by simply leaving the website. You can also use your browser's privacy settings to limit what is shared with third-party services.

Data Retention

We aim to keep information only as long as it is needed for the purpose it was collected. Server logs are retained according to the hosting provider's settings. Messages you send to the site owner, once contact details are published, would be kept only for as long as necessary to respond and to document a correction, after which they would be deleted or archived in a way that limits access.

We do not keep any other personal information, because we do not collect it.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the website and the limited data connected with it. The site is made of static files, which reduces the attack surface compared with sites that run databases and server-side applications. We recommend that the site be served over HTTPS so that the connection between your browser and the server is encrypted.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you believe you have found a security problem with this website, please report it once contact details are available, and we will review it promptly.

Links to Other Websites

The website contains links to external websites, including league sites, governing bodies and other sources of sports information. The special information notice page also contains buttons that lead to google.com. When you click such links, you leave Tony Sport Hub, and the privacy practices of the destination apply. We do not control those websites and are not responsible for their content or data handling.

We encourage you to read the privacy policy of any site you visit, particularly before entering personal information.

Your Rights and Choices

Canadian privacy laws give individuals certain rights over their personal information, which typically include the right to ask what information an organization holds about them, to ask for corrections, and to raise concerns. Because we do not collect personal information through this website, there is generally nothing to access, correct or delete. If you believe we hold information about you, you may contact us and we will make reasonable efforts to respond.

You can also exercise choices in your own browser, such as blocking third-party content, clearing stored data, using private browsing, or limiting what is sent to websites. If you are unhappy with how your information has been handled, you may contact the privacy regulator in your province or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Canadian Privacy Considerations

This website is aimed at readers in Canada. Federal privacy rules for the private sector are set out in the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act, commonly called PIPEDA. Several provinces, including Quebec, Alberta and British Columbia, have their own private-sector privacy laws, and their requirements may differ in detail. We do not claim certification or formal compliance status under any of these laws, and nothing on this page is legal advice.

Our approach is to follow the practical principles those laws share: collect little, be clear about what happens to information, protect it, keep it only as long as necessary, and respond to questions. Because the website does not process personal information beyond routine technical data, we expect few privacy concerns, but we will revisit this assessment if the site changes.

Canada's anti-spam legislation, known as CASL, governs commercial electronic messages. We do not send marketing emails or text messages, and we do not collect email addresses for that purpose.

International Data Transfers

The website is intended for readers in Canada, but the internet is global. Technical data such as your IP address may be processed on servers in other countries, for example by a hosting provider or by Google Fonts. When data is processed outside Canada, it may be subject to the laws of that country, including laws that allow local authorities to request access. We encourage the site owner to choose a hosting provider that is transparent about where it stores data, and to update this policy when the hosting arrangement is finalized.

Security Incidents

If we become aware of a security incident that creates a real risk of significant harm to individuals, we will assess it promptly and take the steps required by the privacy laws that apply, including notifying affected people and the relevant regulator where required. Because we hold very little personal information, we expect such an incident to be unlikely, but we plan for it so that the response is quick and clear.

Children's Privacy

Tony Sport Hub is a general-audience sports information site and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because there are no accounts or forms, a child using the site is not asked for personal details. Parents and guardians who have concerns are welcome to contact us once contact information is available.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the website evolves or as legal requirements change. When we make a material change, we will update the date at the top of the page. We encourage you to review the policy occasionally. If a change would significantly affect how your information is handled, we will highlight it more prominently on the website.

You can review related documents on our Terms and Conditions, Cookie Policy and Disclaimer pages.

Contact Information

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, want to report a concern or wish to make a request, please use the contact details published on our About page.

Contact details have not yet been published on this website. The site owner should add a contact email address to the About page and to this section before launch. Until then, this notice does not provide a way to reach us directly.
