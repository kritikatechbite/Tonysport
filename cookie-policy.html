What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website sends to your browser and that your browser stores on your device. They allow a website to recognize your device on a later visit, remember choices you have made and, in some cases, measure how the website is used. Cookies can be set by the website you are visiting, which are called first-party cookies, or by another organization whose content is embedded in the page, which are called third-party cookies.

Cookies can be "session" cookies, which are deleted when you close your browser, or "persistent" cookies, which stay on your device for a set period of time. Similar technologies include local storage, session storage, pixels, tags and device fingerprinting. In this policy, we use the word cookies to refer to all of them unless we say otherwise.

Our Approach to Cookies

Tony Sport Hub is a static information website. It has no accounts, no shopping cart, no comments and no forms. Because of that, it does not need cookies in order to work. The pages, scripts and style sheets we deliver do not create or read cookies, and they do not store personal data in your browser.

We chose this approach to respect your privacy and to keep the website fast. If our approach changes, we will update this page and our Privacy Policy first.

Essential Cookies

Essential cookies are those a website needs in order to deliver the service you ask for, such as keeping you signed in or remembering items in a basket. This website does not require essential cookies to operate and does not set any.

It is possible that your hosting provider or a security service in front of the website sets a technical cookie to protect against abuse or to balance traffic. Such a cookie would be set by that provider, for security and delivery purposes, and not by the pages we publish. If the site owner activates a service of that kind, this section will be updated to describe it.

Analytics Cookies

Analytics cookies collect information about how visitors use a website, for example which pages are most popular. The website does not currently use analytics cookies, and it does not include analytics scripts.

If we decide to add analytics in the future, we will describe the service we choose, the data it collects, how long the data is kept and how to opt out. Where the law or good practice calls for it, we will ask for your agreement before any non-essential analytics cookies are set. We would prefer a privacy-respecting approach that does not follow people across websites.

Functional Cookies

Functional cookies remember your preferences, such as language or layout. The website does not set functional cookies. Filters on the matches and insights pages, such as the sport selector, work only while the page is open and are not saved in your browser. When you leave or reload the page, the filter returns to its default setting.

Advertising and Targeting Cookies

The website does not display advertising and does not use advertising or targeting cookies. We do not use social media tracking pixels, and we do not share information with advertisers.

Third-Party Cookies and Services

The pages load fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those font files, Google's servers receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type. Google's public documentation describes how it handles this information and whether any cookies are involved. We do not control the practices of Google or any other third party.

The information notice page includes buttons that open google.com. Once you click them, you leave our website, and Google's cookies and policies apply to your visit there.

If we add third-party services

If we add any other embedded content, such as videos, social media posts or maps, those services may set their own cookies. We would list them on this page and give you information about how to control them.

How to Control Cookies in Your Browser

All major browsers let you manage cookies. You can usually:

  • View the cookies stored on your device and delete some or all of them
  • Block all cookies, or only third-party cookies
  • Allow cookies only from websites you choose
  • Clear cookies automatically each time you close your browser
  • Use private or incognito windows, which delete cookies when the window is closed

The steps differ between browsers and devices, so please check the help section of your browser. Be aware that blocking all cookies may stop some websites from working properly. Because this website does not depend on cookies, blocking them should not affect your experience here.

You can also use privacy tools or browser extensions that limit tracking. Some browsers support signals such as Global Privacy Control, and we do not use any tracking that would be affected by such a signal.

Consent Preferences

The website does not currently include a cookie consent banner or a consent management platform, because it does not set cookies that require your consent. We do not claim to operate any consent management system that has not been implemented.

If non-essential cookies are introduced later, a consent tool that lets you accept, decline and change your choices will be added before they are used, and this page will explain how it works.

Cookie Retention

Because the website does not set cookies of its own, there are no cookies of ours to retain. If a hosting provider or security service sets a technical cookie, its lifetime depends on that provider's configuration, and such cookies are typically either session cookies or last a short period.

If we introduce cookies in the future, we will state the name, purpose and typical lifespan of each, and we will keep the lifespan as short as reasonably possible.

Local Storage and Similar Technologies

The website's scripts do not use local storage, session storage or IndexedDB to save personal data. The scripts only handle interactions you can see, such as opening the mobile menu, filtering lists and revealing page sections as you scroll. Those interactions happen entirely in your browser and are not sent back to us.

Canadian Considerations

In Canada, privacy law expects organizations to be open about the use of tracking technologies and to obtain meaningful consent when personal information is collected for purposes beyond what a user would reasonably expect. Quebec's private-sector privacy law has particular requirements for technologies that can identify, locate or profile people. Because this website does not use tracking technologies, those requirements are unlikely to be triggered, but we will review them before making any change. This page is general information and not legal advice.

We may change this policy as the website changes or as the law develops. We will update the date at the top of the page when we do. If a change would affect how cookies are used on this website, we will make the change clear on the site and, where required, ask for your consent before the change takes effect.

A Plain-Language Summary

If you only have a minute, here is what you need to know:

  • The website does not set cookies of its own
  • There are no accounts, forms or comments that would require them
  • There is no analytics or advertising on the website as delivered
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which is a third-party service
  • Filters and menus work in the page only and are not saved
  • You can control cookies in your browser at any time

Why We Explain This Even Without Cookies

Many websites place a cookie banner at the top of every page. We have not, because we do not set cookies that would call for one. Even so, we think it is useful to say plainly what the website does and does not do, so that you do not have to guess. A visitor is entitled to know whether a site is tracking them, and the honest answer here is that we are not.

We also know that websites change over time. A feature added next year could change the answer, and the policy would need to change with it. For this reason, we date this page and ask the site owner to review it any time the website's technology or hosting changes.

Questions You Might Have

Does the website remember me? No. We do not recognize returning visitors, because we do not set identifiers.

Will I see a cookie banner? Not at present. If we add non-essential cookies, you will be offered a choice before they are used.

Can I use the website with cookies turned off? Yes. Every page works without cookies.

Does my browser store anything about my visit? Your browser keeps its ordinary history and cache of pages and images. That is a normal browser feature controlled by you, not a cookie set by us. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

What about my hosting provider? Providers may keep server logs that include your IP address. Those logs are described in our Privacy Policy and are not cookies.

Do Not Track and Browser Signals

Some browsers send signals such as "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control to tell websites that you do not want to be tracked. Because this website does not track visitors, those signals have no effect on what the website does. If we add tracking technologies in the future, we will explain how we respond to such signals.

Examples of What Could Change

To make this policy concrete, here are examples of changes that would require us to revise it and to inform you:

  • Adding a website analytics service that uses cookies or similar identifiers
  • Embedding a video player, social media feed or map from another provider
  • Introducing a newsletter sign-up or a user account
  • Displaying advertising or sponsor messages that use tracking technology
  • Moving the website to a hosting or security provider that sets its own cookies

In every case, we would describe the change here, update our Privacy Policy and, where required, ask for your consent before any non-essential cookie is placed on your device. We would also keep the number and lifespan of cookies to the minimum needed.

You are always welcome to ask us what we do with information from your visit. If you cannot find the answer on this page, please use the details on the About page.
